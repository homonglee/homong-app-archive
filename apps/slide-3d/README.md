# 슬라이드 랩 · 경사면 실험실

Archive: https://homong-app.com/slide-3d/

Standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript physics lab, requiring no external libraries, build step or secrets. Preserves Sites source commit 80c8004b13b98a57a5a61b37434da711e2f354ed.

Adjust angle, mass and friction; compare frictionless motion, inspect force vectors and velocity charts. Static and kinetic friction coefficients are assumed equal; air resistance and rotation are ignored.
